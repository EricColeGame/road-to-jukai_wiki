Navigating the murky paths of the Sanroku Forest can quickly overwhelm an unprepared party, making a clear guide for the **Road to Jukai quests** essential for your survival. Completing the **Road to Jukai quests** requires careful resource management, puzzle-solving, and decisive combat against punishing folklore bosses. ## Dungeon Preparation and Essential Spells

Before stepping foot into the Sanroku Jukai Forest, your squad must be properly outfitted. By this stage in *Labyrinth of Zangetsu*, your casters should have access to at least Tier 2 or Tier 3 magic. Spell slots are bound to specific tiers, allowing you to cast lower-tier utility spells freely without depleting your high-damage boss-killing reserves.

According to veteran player experience, utility magic drastically speeds up dungeon navigation while preserving your consumable items.

| Spell / Tool | Class & Tier | Primary Purpose | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Increase Perception** | Cleric (Tier 2) | Highlights hidden objects | Prompts party members to spot hidden root tiles automatically |
| **Light** | Cleric (Tier 3) | Illuminates dark zones | Provides permanent illumination until exiting the labyrinth |
| **Torches** | Consumable Item | Temporary light source | Backup option if your Cleric lacks Tier 3 spell slots |
| **Fire AoE Magic** | Mage (Tier 2) | Area-of-effect damage | Exploits elemental weaknesses on standard plant enemies |
| **Gale** | Mage (Tier 4) | Buffs party hit count | Adds +1 hit per attack for 10 turns during prolonged boss battles |

You will encounter numerous dark tiles in the eastern reaches of the forest. Bringing a steady light source prevents party ambushes and keeps trap damage to a minimum.

## The Feather Hunt: Northwest Forest Sector

The main objective in the opening section of the **Road to Jukai quests** involves recovering lost items scattered throughout the Northwest (NW) zone. The forest floor is littered with tangled root tiles that trigger interactive prompts when approached.

### Northwest Exploration Steps

1. **Activate Perception:** Cast the Cleric's Tier 2 perception boost immediately upon entering the floor.
2. **Search the Root Tiles:** Thoroughly search every tangled root formation in the northwest quadrant.
3. **Handle Encounters:** Some root searches trigger plant ambush encounters. Use Tier 2 fire magic to clear them in a single round.
4. **Identify Before Using:** Ensure you appraise and identify all three feathers in your inventory. This contributes directly toward the 140-item catalog achievement.

| Item Name | Sector Location | Tile Type | Critical Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lost Feather A** | Northwest (NW) | Root Search Tile | Must be identified before clearing the quest |
| **Lost Feather B** | Northwest (NW) | Root Search Tile | Guarded by weak plant-type monsters |
| **Lost Feather C** | Northwest (NW) | Root Search Tile | Found near the northern perimeter wall |

Once all three feathers are safely stored and identified, make your way toward the Southeast (SE) corner of the map to locate the NPC waiting for them.

## The Tengu Dilemma: Choice, Combat, and Missables

When you arrive at the Southeast corner, you will encounter the mythical **Tengu**. This interaction is a critical turning point for the **Road to Jukai quests**, containing the only truly missable achievement in the entire game. 

Before speaking to him, it is strongly recommended that you exit the labyrinth to trigger a manual save in town.

### Tengu Strategy & Stats Breakdown

If you are pursuing 100% completion, you must choose to lie to Tengu by telling him you did not find his feathers. When he presses the issue, refuse him again to initiate combat.

| Boss Action | Threat Level | Target Type | Counter Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AoE Wind Magic** | High | Whole Party | Cast group wards or immediately top off fragile Mages |
| **Single Target Strike** | Deadly | Single Ally | Maintain full HP; heal allies immediately if they hit 0 HP |
| **Standard Melee** | Moderate | Front Row | Keep defensive buffs active on your primary tank |

*Note on Downed Characters:* In *Labyrinth of Zangetsu*, reaching 0 HP does not cause permanent death instantly unless the character suffers an additional unmitigated blow. Apply healing magic immediately to bring fallen heroes back into the fight. Defeating Tengu awards the **Magic Feathers**, granting safe passage through the hazardous Northeast sector.

## Navigating the Dark Sector and Portals

With the Magic Feathers in your inventory, head toward the Northeast (NE) sector. This sector is heavily shrouded in darkness and packed with floor hazards that chip away at party health if crossed recklessly.

| Hazard / Feature | Sector Location | Function | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Darkness Tiles** | Northeast | Disables map vision | Maintain Tier 3 Cleric Light spell |
| **Floor Spike Traps** | Northeast | Deals minor continuous damage | Step across deliberately; heal periodically |
| **Teal Portals** | Central/East | Teleportation network | Transports your squad to South/Center zone |
| **Transition Gate** | East-Central | Floor transition | Leads directly to the Boss Chamber |

The teal portals throughout the labyrinth act as shortcuts, consistently routing your party back to the south-central forest clearing. Locate the marked eastern passage to transition into the inner sanctum.

## Boss Encounter: Yatsutsuki Spider

Crossing the threshold triggers an automatic floor transition save. The inner chamber is compact, featuring two secret rooms. The hidden room in the Northwest corner holds a **single-use healing spring** that restores your entire party's HP (though it does not restore spell charges).

Progressing through the **Road to Jukai quests** culminates in a showdown against the colossal **Yatsutsuki**, an eight-legged monstrosity that punishes purely physical combat setups.

| Boss Phase | Boss Action | Tactical Impact | Best Counterplay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Buff Phase** | Harden (Self-Buff) | Drastically reduces incoming physical hit rates | Rely primarily on direct magic burst damage |
| **Sustain Phase**| Passive Regeneration | Restores a portion of HP at the end of each turn | Maximize burst per turn to outpace healing |
| **Offensive Phase**| Multi-Target Sweep | Chips away at front-line defenders | Cycle mid-tier group heals every 2 turns |

If your party has unlocked Tier 4 spells, open the fight by casting **Gale** to grant extra hit counts to your front line. If Yatsutsuki stacks *Harden*, physical weapons will struggle to land clean hits. Rely on your casters to burn their remaining spell tiers to deplete the boss's HP pool rapidly.

## Cleansing the Forest: Stake Mechanics

Following Yatsutsuki's defeat, talk with the surviving NPCs standing near the clearing. They will reward your efforts by providing **Purification Stakes**, which are required to finalize the **Road to Jukai quests**.

| Step | Action Required | Danger Rating | Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 1** | Locate Gray Smoke plumes | Safe | Approach the interactable gray miasma tiles |
| **Step 2** | Avoid Black Smoke tiles | Hazardous | Do not step on black smoke; these are lethal traps |
| **Step 3** | Drive the Stake (x3) | Moderate | Each drive inflicts ~10 recoil damage randomly to party |
| **Step 4** | Trigger Final NPC Dialogue | Safe | Speak with NPCs to unlock the next labyrinth in town |

Ensure every party member has at least 15–20 HP remaining before driving a stake, as the lingering backlash can accidentally knock out low-health characters. Once all three gray smoke plumes are cleansed, return to the hub town to open your path forward. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I complete the Road to Jukai quests without fighting Tengu?
Yes. You can peacefully hand over the three lost feathers to Tengu in the Southeast corner. He will reward you with the Magic Feathers directly. However, doing so will permanently lock you out of the missable combat achievement for that playthrough.

### What should I do if my physical attacks keep missing Yatsutsuki?
Yatsutsuki regularly casts *Harden*, heavily degrading your party's physical accuracy. To counter this, switch your main damage output to Tier 2 and Tier 3 elemental offensive spells, or cast accuracy-boosting buffs like *Gale* before the boss sets up defenses.

### Do I lose my feathers if I identify them before finishing the quest?
No. Identifying quest items at the shop or via character skills merely logs them in your compendium for the 140-item collection achievement. They remain in your inventory as functional quest items until used or turned in.

### Why am I taking damage when cleansing the gray smoke?
Driving the purification stakes into the gray smoke causes a magical backlash that deals roughly 10 points of direct damage to random party members. Always heal your squad above this damage threshold before activating each stake.
