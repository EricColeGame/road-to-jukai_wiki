## Dungeon Overview and Core Mechanics

The Path to Jukai (often called the Road to Jukai) serves as the game's opening trial. While the layout is relatively compact compared to late-game maps, early-game parties lack the defensive stats and high-tier magic needed to recover easily from careless mistakes. 

Unlike traditional dungeon crawlers featuring invisible random battles, encounters here appear directly on the map as visible clouds of fog. Learning how to identify these hazards ahead of time will save your squad from sudden wipes.

| Encounter Fog Type | Threat Level | Typical Enemies | Recommended Player Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grey Fog** | Low to Moderate | Standard trash mobs, Goblins | Clear normally; safe to use the auto/repeat command |
| **Red Fog** | High to Lethal | Mini-bosses, Moto Inu, Rare Spawns | Save before entering; top off party HP; prepare single-target magic |
| **Event Icons** | Variable / Story | Guards, Optional Bosses (Hettsui Yurei) | Read prompts carefully; review alignment choices |

Almost all enemy spawns—including standard red fog encounters—respawn when you exit the labyrinth or move between floors. According to community reports on the [Labyrinth of Zangetsu Steam Community Hub](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1922980/Labyrinth_of_Zangetsu/), treating red fog encounters with caution early on is the best way to prevent losing hard-earned dungeon progress.

## Step-by-Step Road to Jukai Walkthrough

Navigating the area requires careful movement, uncovering hidden walls, and securing the southern gate shortcut before venturing deeper into the northeastern region.

```
[ NW: Hettsui Yurei ] -------- [ Central Guard Post ] -------- [ NE: Sanroku Jukai Exit ]
          |                              |                                |
          |                     [ Large Goblin Mob ]               [ Ferryman / Warp ]
          |                              |                                |
[ SW: Starting Area ] -------- [ Shortcut Gate ] -------------------------+
```

### 1. The Starting Area and Central Crossing
When you first enter from the southwestern sector, take your time mapping every dead end. If a corridor seems to end abruptly without connecting to an adjacent hallway, press your **Examine/Interact** button against the wall to search for hidden illusory paths. 

Work your way toward the center of the floor. Here, you will encounter friendly guards stationed along the road who offer dialogue warnings about a massive group of monsters congregating just to the west.

### 2. Clearing the Goblin Ambush
Directly west of the guard post lies a mandatory grey encounter accompanied by a unique story warning. This encounter pits your party against a massive horde:

- **Enemy Composition:** Approximately 10 standard goblins accompanied by a Goblin Boss.
- **Backline Danger:** Standard goblins possess ranged attacks that can strike your back row. Keep squishy casters and healers shielded or topped off.
- **Boss Mechanics:** The Goblin Boss operates purely as a melee combatant. Because of the sheer number of frontline minions, he will take several turns to reach melee range, giving you plenty of time to thin the herd.

### 3. Unlocking the Shortcut
Once the goblin swarm is cleared, immediately proceed south to unlock the one-way gate. Opening this shortcut connects the central thoroughfare directly back to the starting sector, drastically shortening your travel time if you need to retreat to town for rest or ink purification.

### Tackling the Northwest Event (Hettsui Yurei)
In the far northwest tile, you will discover an interactive prompt. Choosing **"Yes"** initiates an optional mini-boss battle against **Hettsui Yurei**. 
- Physical weapons deal minimal damage against this spirit.
- Direct your spellcasters to cast their strongest elemental magic.
- This battle is entirely repeatable upon re-entering the floor, making it a viable target for early-game grinding once your spell economy stabilizes.

## Essential Combat and Party Management Tips

Success in *Labyrinth of Zangetsu* requires smart resource management. Applying the following fundamental rules during your **Road to Jukai walkthrough** run will keep your party alive and well-aligned.

### The Alignment Choice Dilemma
Occasionally, an encounter begins with three interaction prompts: **Attack**, **Wait**, or **Ignore**. Player experience reveals that these choices directly influence your characters' moral alignments:
- **Attack:** High chance to shift party members toward **Evil**.
- **Ignore:** High chance to shift party members toward **Good**.
- **Wait:** Safe option that causes **no alignment changes**.

To prevent accidental class disqualifications or stat penalties, consistently choose **Wait** unless you are intentionally tailoring an alignment change.

### Combat Efficiency and Ink Purification
To speed up turn resolution, use the **Repeat** function at the start of each combat round to automatically execute your previous action queue. However, monitor your party's maximum health pool closely. 

If characters are damaged by ink-tainted foes, their maximum HP will decrease over time (known as being **Afflicted by Ink**). This ailment cannot be cured by basic resting spells in the field; you must visit the town temple and pay a nominal fee to cleanse the ink.

| System Mechanic | In-Game Effect | Best Practice / Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Afflicted by Ink** | Reduces maximum party HP progressively | Purify at the town temple after every full exploration run |
| **Repeat Command** | Repeats previous combat turns instantly | Use during standard grey fog battles to save time |
| **Floor Boundary Save** | Autosaves game status upon swapping floors | Use floor transitions before engaging dangerous red fog |

## Leveling Mechanics and the Reset Strategy

Level progression in *Labyrinth of Zangetsu* contains an element of randomness. When characters level up at the inn, primary stats can randomly increase—and occasionally decrease.

1. **How Autosaving Works:** The game does not commit your stat gains immediately when you view the level-up screen. However, it *does* autosave whenever you change dungeon floors or enter the labyrinth.
2. **Stat Optimization Trick:** If an essential character suffers a severe stat loss upon leveling up in town, you can immediately force-close the application (Alt+F4 on PC) before taking actions that trigger an autosave.
3. **Stat Equalization Warning:** The game naturally attempts to balance character attributes relative to their current level curve. Artificially forcing max stats early on often increases the likelihood of negative stat rolls on subsequent level-ups. Use this method sparingly, primarily when qualifying for an urgent class change.

## Transitioning to Sanroku Jukai Forest

The primary objective of this **Road to Jukai walkthrough** is reaching the northeastern exit.

| Landmark | Location | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ferryman** | Northeast (near exit) | Provides instant, safe transit back to town |
| **Sanroku Jukai Entrance** | Northeast Portal | Unlocks the second labyrinth |
| **Fast Travel Node** | Zone Boundary | Registers the new forest zone on your world map |

Before speaking with the Ferryman to return home, **step through the northeastern threshold into Sanroku Jukai Forest at least once**. Crossing the threshold registers the forest on your town teleport directory, allowing you to bypass the Road to Jukai entirely on subsequent excursions.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Where is the exit in the Road to Jukai walkthrough?
The main exit leading into the next labyrinth, Sanroku Jukai Forest, is located in the far **northeastern corner** of the map. Right beside the zone transition, you will also find the Ferryman, who can transport you directly back to town.

### How do I cure the ink affliction reducing my max HP?
Ink affliction cannot be cured with standard consumable herbs or regular field magic. You must return to the town hub and visit the **Temple**, where you can purify your party members for a modest monetary fee.

### Should I fight every red fog enemy on the Path to Jukai?
Early on, it is safer to bypass red fog encounters unless your party is fully rested. Enemies like the Moto Inu can deal upward of 20 magic damage in a single hit, which is enough to eliminate backline characters instantly. Always ensure you have unlocked the southern shortcut before taking unnecessary risks.
