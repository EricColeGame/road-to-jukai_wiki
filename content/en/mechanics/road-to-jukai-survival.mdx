## Core Gameplay Loop: The Anatomy of a Night Shift

Your overarching objective across all six shifts is deceptively straightforward: pick up fares in isolated towns, drive winding mountain routes, drop passengers off at designated locations, and survive until the sun rises. However, the environmental pressure increases with each completed run. 

Every night introduces denser fog, strange radio anomalies, and passengers whose humanity is entirely questionable. You must balance vehicle controls while constantly monitoring cabin behavior.

```
[Accept Dispatch Request] ➔ [Inspect & Seat Passenger] ➔ [Navigate Mountain Routes] ➔ [Manage In-Cabin Anomalies] ➔ [Drop-off & Refuel]
```

## Passenger Screening and Backseat Anomaly Identification

Not every fare waiting on the dark curbs of rural Japan is a living human being. One of the most critical aspects of your **Road to Jukai survival** run involves screening potential riders before unlocking the passenger door.

Look for subtle visual and auditory tells. Human passengers exhibit natural breathing rhythms, respond logically to dialogue prompts, and possess visible reflections in your taxi's mirrors. Malevolent spirits or cursed entities often mimic passenger behavior but display glaring supernatural flaws.

### Passenger Classification Matrix

| Fare Type | Visual Indicators | Audio Behavior | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Commuter** | Normal breathing, checking phone/watch | Clear voice, asks for route adjustments | Accept fare; drive smoothly |
| **The Lost Soul** | Pale skin, soaking wet clothes, averted gaze | Whispering, repetitive weeping | Accept with caution; do not look in rearview mirror |
| **Forest Doppelgänger** | Glitching limbs, lack of mirror reflection | Distorted audio, delayed speech responses | Keep doors locked; accelerate immediately |
| **Jukai Yurei** | Floating posture, static around frame | Complete silence or backwards audio | Deploy cabin salt/talismans; purge vehicle |

Community reports from early playtesters indicate that allowing an entity into the backseat triggers a rapid sanity drain. If you accidentally seat a non-human entity, you must maintain a steady speed while utilizing vehicle countermeasures to force them out before reaching dense forest corridors.

## Advanced Survival Tactics and Pro Tips

Achieving total mastery over the game requires subtle micro-management decisions behind the wheel. Incorporate these proven tactics to ensure consistent **Road to Jukai survival**:

1. **Master the Rearview Glance**: Never stare directly into the rearview mirror for more than two seconds continuously. Staring accelerates panic and triggers entity aggression. Glance quickly to confirm passenger state, then return your eyes to the road.
2. **Conserve Vehicle Battery**: When idling at isolated drop-off points, switch off auxiliary cabin lights and the radio. Battery drain forces manual push-starts in complete darkness, exposing you to ambient forest threats.
3. **Listen to Environmental Cues**: The vehicle radio serves as an early warning detection system. Static bursts and rhythmic clicks indicate a high-level entity in close proximity along the road.
4. **Maintain Moderate Speeds**: While speeding away from horrifying apparitions is tempting, high speeds on wet Japanese mountain asphalt cause severe drift and wheel lock, leading to fatal crashes.
5. **Manage Your Salt Supplies**: Keep protective talismans and purification salt replenished at regional gas stations. Sprinkling salt along the back floorboards buys precious seconds during in-cabin hauntings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important mechanic for Road to Jukai survival?
The most critical mechanic is cabin sanity management combined with vigilant passenger screening. Failing to inspect fares before unlocking your taxi doors allows supernatural entities into your backseat, which rapidly depletes sanity and causes fatal driving distractions.

### How do I identify a fake or spectral passenger?
Look for unnatural visual anomalies such as a lack of reflection in mirrors, erratic breathing patterns, glowing or distorted eyes, and disjointed voice lines. If a fare does not resemble a standard commuter, do not unlock the doors.

### What should I do if my car stalls inside the forest?
Turn off your headlights immediately to avoid drawing attention from surrounding forest entities. Engage the emergency brake, restart the ignition using the manual crank, and keep cabin interior lights extinguished until the engine turns over.

### Can you complete the game without using the paper map?
While the digital dashboard GPS functions during early shifts, later nights introduce heavy electromagnetic interference that disrupts electronics. Relying on physical road signs and the paper map is essential for late-game navigation.
