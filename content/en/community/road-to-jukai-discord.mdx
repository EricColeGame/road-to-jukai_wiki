## Safety, Verification, and Avoiding Scams

With anticipation building for upcoming indie horror titles, fake alpha test invites, unofficial server mirrors, and malicious direct messages (DMs) can target eager fans. Protecting your account requires following standard security protocols when participating in any gaming server.

| Safety Protocol | Recommended Action | Risk Level Prevented |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DM Verification** | Disable direct messages from server members | High (Phishing, fake beta test links) |
| **Two-Factor Auth (2FA)** | Enable 2FA on your personal Discord account | High (Account hijacking) |
| **Link Clicking** | Only click links shared in official `#announcements` | Critical (Malware, credential harvesting) |
| **Staff Verification** | Check for distinct moderator and developer badges | Medium (Impersonation scams) |

Developer staff and moderators will never message you privately to ask for passwords, payment details, or personal login tokens to access exclusive playtests.

## How to Get the Most Out of the Community

To maximize your experience inside the **Road to Jukai Discord**, consider actively participating in developer-led community events. Indie horror studios often host interactive community activities during development cycles.

### Actionable Community Tips

1. **Set Up Custom Notifications:** Mute general chatter channels and leave notifications enabled for `#announcements` to receive immediate alerts regarding release dates and demo drops.
2. **Utilize Discord's Search Syntax:** Use search filters like `from:developer_name` or `has:image` in technical channels to find verified developer answers quickly without asking redundant questions.
3. **Participate in Beta Feedback Windows:** When public or closed demos go live, submit structured feedback forms containing your system specs, frame rate metrics, and encountered glitches.
4. **Tag Spoilers Appropriately:** Use `||spoiler text||` formatting when discussing sensitive narrative elements, character fates, or hidden endings to respect new players.
