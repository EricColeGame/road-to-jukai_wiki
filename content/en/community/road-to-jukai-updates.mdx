The transition to *Road to Jukai* marks a deliberate sharpening of the game's cultural atmosphere and thematic focus, explicitly referencing the historic Jukai (Sea of Trees) near Mount Fuji.

The game tasks players with operating a night-shift taxi along desolate, narrow mountain roads.
