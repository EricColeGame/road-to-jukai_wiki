## Core Pillars of the Jukai Journey: The Three Vows & Lineages

Entering Jukai means committing your daily life to non-harming, compassion, and continuous awakening. When entering the program with authorized **Road to Jukai codes**, students engage in deep intellectual, ethical, and creative assignments.

The three primary practical projects required during this training period include:

1. **Sewing the Rakusu:** Hand-stitching a miniature Buddhist vestment (the *rakusu*) using traditional measurement patterns, repeating the Three Refuges with every stitch.
2. **Documenting Ancestral Lineages:** Hand-writing or charting three distinct lineages—the historical Zen masters, the often-overlooked women ancestors of the Dharma, and your personal lineage of direct spiritual influencers.
3. **Precept Study and Vow Living:** Examining personal ethics through reflective journaling on all sixteen Bodhisattva Precepts.

### Overview of the 16 Bodhisattva Precepts

| Category | Precept Focus | Everyday Practice Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Three Treasures** | Buddha, Dharma, Sangha | Taking refuge in wisdom, truth, and community |
| **The Three Pure Precepts** | Cease Evil, Do Good, Do Good for Others | Directing all intentional actions toward universal benefit |
| **The Ten Grave Precepts** | Non-Killing, Non-Stealing, Clear Mind, etc. | Living ethically in relationships, speech, and consumption |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What should I do if my Road to Jukai codes fail to work?
If your registration or discount code displays an error during checkout, ensure that you are logged into your active membership account. If issues persist, contact the registrar at `onlineprograms@upaya.org` to request a manual fee adjustment or replacement access link.

### Are there scholarships available if I cannot afford standard program fees?
Yes. Most centers offer a dedicated scholarship application for practitioners experiencing financial hardship. Applying for financial aid allows you to receive customized fee waivers and scholarship **Road to Jukai codes** that reduce tuition to an accessible level.

### What happens if I miss a scheduled live discussion session?
All bi-monthly meetings (held Sundays from 2:00 p.m. to 3:15 p.m. Mountain Time) are recorded and uploaded directly to the private student resource dashboard. Registered students can review recorded lectures and discussions at any time throughout the year.

### Can international students participate in the online Jukai preparation program?
Yes, the interactive digital format accommodates practitioners worldwide. While live attendance is encouraged for the bi-monthly meetings, international participants can utilize recorded materials and remote sewing circles to complete their requirements before traveling or joining the final ceremony virtually.
