## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I find the hidden radio frequencies in Road to Jukai?
Hidden radio stations can be discovered by manually sweeping the tuner when approaching transmission towers or old bridges. When static suddenly shifts into rhythmic clicking or muffled dialogue, leave the dial on that exact frequency to log the audio secret.

### Can all Road to Jukai secrets be unlocked in a single playthrough?
No. Because the game utilizes a branching six-night narrative structure with mutually exclusive passenger choices, viewing every secret and ending requires at least two to three complete playthroughs with different route decisions.

### What should I do when the taxi engine stalls in dark zones?
If your vehicle stalls in an unlit section of the road, turn off all auxiliary electronics immediately to preserve battery. Wait three seconds for the starter to cool down, crank the ignition once, and immediately shift into low gear while keeping your eyes on the road ahead.
