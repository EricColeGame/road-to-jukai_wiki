## Dungeon Mechanics: Visible Encounters and Fog Types

Unlike classic dungeon crawlers that rely purely on invisible random step encounters, this labyrinth telegraphs its battles through visible fog tiles on the grid. Paying attention to the color of the fog allows you to prepare your party before stepping onto a combat tile.

Understanding your enemy's threat tier before triggering combat lets you buff, heal, or decide whether to return to town to bank your experience.

| Fog Type | Threat Level | Encounter Composition | Recommended Preparation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grey Fog** | Low to Moderate | Standard mobs, roaming swarms, minor goblins | Basic attack order, reserve MP for bosses |
| **Red Fog** | High to Deadly | Elites, rare spawns (e.g., Moto Inu), mini-bosses | Full party buffing, save state/floor swap |
| **Event Tiles** | Variable | Scripted dialogues, optional challenges (Hettsui Yurei) | Top off HP, ensure offensive caster has spell slots |

According to player experience and community reports, non-story red fog encounters respawn whenever you transition between floors or return to town. If you run into a brutal red encounter that taxes your party's resources, consider saving by swapping labyrinth floors before engaging.

## Breakdown of Key Road to Jukai Enemies

The early labyrinth throws a mix of horde encounters, backline snipers, and high-damage casters at your squad. Identifying target priority among **Road to Jukai enemies** prevents unnecessary casualties during long dungeon runs.

```
                  [ Frontline Mobs ]
                 /                  \
   [ Goblin Swarm / Boss ]       [ Moto Inu (Caster) ]
        |                              |
  Melee focus / High count       Single-target nuke (20+ DMG)
        |                              |
   Protect Backline              Burst Down First
```

### 1. The Goblin Swarm and Goblin Leader
Near the center-west section of the map, past the cautionary guards, you will encounter a massive scripted grey fog horde. This fight features roughly ten basic goblin units led by a tougher boss goblin.

* **Behavior:** Standard goblins can hit any row in your formation, making your low-constitution Wizard an easy target.
* **The Boss:** Restricted strictly to melee range. The leader will slowly advance as you clear the front row.
* **Counter Strategy:** Keep your front row above 50% HP at all times and focus down individual goblins to lower their action economy quickly.

### 2. Moto Inu (Red Fog Elite)
Moto Inu represents one of the earliest lethal **Road to Jukai enemies** you can encounter in the zone. As a spellcasting beast, it poses a direct threat to any character lacking magical defense.

* **Damage Output:** Moto Inu can deal upwards of 20 damage in a single cast to a random party member.
* **Danger Level:** At starting levels, 20 damage is enough to instantly knock out or kill your spellcasters.
* **Counter Strategy:** Prioritize fast physical skills or burst spells to eliminate Moto Inu before it casts on turn one.

### 3. Hettsui Yurei (Optional Northwestern Encounter)
Tucked away in the northwest corner is an optional event tile. Choosing to interact and answer "Yes" triggers a battle with Hettsui Yurei, an ethereal spirit with high physical resilience.

* **Strengths:** High physical evasion and defense against standard weapon strikes.
* **Weaknesses:** Highly vulnerable to offensive elemental magic.
* **Tactic:** Have your primary caster spam attack spells while physical fighters defend or use tactical consumables.

## Essential Combat Tactics for Early Labyrinths

Surviving encounters with **Road to Jukai enemies** requires smart party management and an understanding of the game's unique alignment and corruption systems.

### Handling Encounter Options: Attack, Wait, or Ignore
Certain battles prompt you with a situational choice before turn one. Your choice directly influences character alignment:

* **Attack:** Can shift characters toward **Evil**.
* **Ignore:** Can shift characters toward **Good**.
* **Wait:** Completely neutral; does not alter party alignment.

To preserve class requirements and avoid accidental alignment shifts, community consensus strongly advises selecting **Wait** whenever these prompts appear.

### Managing Ink Affliction
During combat, enemy attacks can inflict "Ink Affliction," which directly lowers a character's maximum HP pool. This debuff persists even after the battle concludes.

| Affliction Status | Impact on Stats | Recovery Method | Location | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Afflicted by Ink** | Max HP temporarily reduced | Purification Ritual | Town Temple | Minor Gold Fee |
| **Fallen / Dead** | Incapacitated | Resurrection Ritual | Town Temple | Moderate Gold Fee |
| **Stat Loss (Level-up)** | Potential random stat drop | Save-scum via floor reload | Labyrinth Staircase | Free (Requires reload) |

Always visit the temple immediately upon returning to town to cleanse Ink Afflictions so your party enters future battles at peak capacity.

## Route Walkthrough and Navigation Tips

The layout of the map is compact, but missing hidden shortcuts or rushing ahead blindly can leave your party stranded without resources.

```
[Start Area] ---> [Guards / Warning] ---> [Goblin Swarm (West)]
                                                |
                                        [Unlock South Shortcut]
                                                |
[Sanroku Jukai Exit (NE)] <--- [Ferryman] <--- [Central Path]
          |
    (Touch zone to 
   unlock Fast Travel)
```

1. **Speak with the Guards:** Located near the center; they give advance warning about the goblin swarm to the west.
2. **Clear the Horde:** Defeat the goblin wave, then unlock the southern shortcut door to ensure an easy retreat path.
3. **Handle Red Fog with Caution:** If you spot red fog, make sure your formation is fully healed before stepping onto the tile.
4. **Locate the Ferryman:** Found near the northeast corner, the ferryman provides a direct ride back to town.
5. **Tag the Exit:** Before using the ferryman, step into the northeast exit leading to the **Sanroku Jukai Forest**. Entering the next labyrinth unlocks it as a permanent fast-travel point from town.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I beat the Moto Inu red fog enemy?
Moto Inu relies on heavy single-target magic that deals roughly 20 damage. To counter it, ensure your frontliners taunt or dish out immediate burst damage on turn one. If your Wizard is low on HP, have them defend or use consumable healing items before the caster acts.

### Can I run past Road to Jukai enemies to reach the exit?
Yes. Because regular encounters are tied to visible fog tiles, you can navigate around certain grey and red fogs if the corridor allows. However, defeating key swarms unlocks crucial shortcuts that make future dungeon runs much safer.

### Why is my party's max HP dropping during combat?
When fighting certain **Road to Jukai enemies**, your characters may become "Afflicted by Ink." This mechanic temporarily caps maximum HP until you purify the character at the town temple for a small gold fee.

### What should I choose when the combat prompt asks to Attack, Wait, or Ignore?
Unless you are intentionally trying to shift a character's alignment toward Good or Evil, always select **Wait**. It leaves your party alignments untouched while immediately proceeding to standard combat.
